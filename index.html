This is what you're after

This whole planet's worth of toy cars has been sitting out here for years. Rust, dust, leaves, flat tyres, a few of them on their roof. Nobody has come back for any of them.

Except one. Somebody still washes it. Exactly one car out there is clean, and in a lot where nothing reflects, it is the only thing that catches the light.

How to play

One car is spotless. Everything else is detail.

  1. WASD walks, Shift runs. On a phone, drag the left side.
  2. Drag to look. Space drops you down among the cars.
  3. Click a car to grab it, click again for the verdict. Q puts it back.
  4. Three 💡 hints per round. Each one points you the way for eight seconds — nothing else.
  5. Refreshing changes nothing. Same planet, same clock.
