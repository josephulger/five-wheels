Every car on the planet has four wheels. Exactly one has five.

How to play

There is no hint system. That is the game.

  1. Walk with WASD; hold Shift to run. On a phone, drag the left side like a joystick.
  2. Hold the mouse button and drag to swing the camera — the car under your cursor lights up.
  3. Press Space to drop down among the cars, where wheels can actually be counted.
  4. Click a car, or press Enter, and you pick it up for a look. Click again for the verdict right away — the wrong ones get tossed over your shoulder, and that costs nothing but time. Q puts a car back.
  5. Stuck? You get one hint: the 💡 button (or H) shows an arrow pointing toward the car for ten seconds. Free, but the share text tells on you.
  6. No do-overs: refreshing the page brings back the same planet, the same clock and your spent hint. A fresh planet comes when you find the car.
